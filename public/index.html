What's Happening Here?

Drifting is a technique where you intentionally oversteer your car to make it lose traction and slide around corners. This was popularized by media like the Fast and Furious movies, Initial D, and has a rich history in Japanese motorsports.

Like the psychos we are, we brought that culture over here. Cars like the G35 are perfect for making drift builds because of their rear-wheel drive layout, balanced weight, and their engines. While not particularly the most popular cars (and now our reputation is ruined becuase most Infiniti drivers cant get their s**t together), Infinitis have proven to be very capable drift cars with their close relation to the Nissan Skyline, with Infinitis even being sold as Skylines in Japan.

Want to get into drifting? The best place to start is finding a cheap, rear-wheel drive car with good power, weight distro, and especially a manual transmission. You can find cars like a MK-3 Toyota Supra, an Infiniti G35 or G37, or some good Hondas for 5-10 grand if you look at the right places. The hard part is getting all the fun aftermarket parts.
